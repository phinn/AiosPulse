Get help with AiosPulse

AiosPulse turns your App Store Connect account into one daily briefing — impressions, page views, conversion and downloads, with funnel breakage called out in plain language. Local-first: your credentials never leave your Mac.

Getting started

Connect your App Store Connect account

  1. In App Store Connect, go to Users and Access → Integrations → Keys and generate an App Store Connect API key with the Developer role.
  2. Note the Key ID and Issuer ID, and download the .p8 private key file.
  3. In AiosPulse, open Settings → App Store Connect and paste all three. The key is stored in your macOS Keychain and used to sign requests locally.
  4. Press Collect now — your first briefing appears within a minute or two, depending on catalogue size.

No account yet? Try the demo

AiosPulse launches with built-in sample data. Every view — briefing, funnel diagnostics, territory charts, AI analyst — works on first launch with zero configuration, so you can evaluate the full product before connecting anything.

FAQ

Where is my API key stored?

In the macOS Keychain on this Mac only. AiosPulse signs App Store Connect API requests (ES256) locally. There is no AiosPulse server, no account system, and no telemetry — nothing about your store data leaves this Mac.

The dashboard shows sample data / numbers look like a demo

You are viewing the built-in sample dataset. Open Settings → App Store Connect, add your API key, and press Collect now to switch to your real data.

Collection finished but yesterday is missing

Apple reports sales and analytics with a delay of roughly 24–48 hours, and the daily pipeline has no fixed clock — the newest day usually lands the next morning. When yesterday isn't published yet, AiosPulse shows a note at the top of the briefing; on the next scheduled collection the day is filled in automatically. If it still looks stale, press Collect now.

What does the funnel "breakage" warning mean?

When impressions are not converting into page views, or page views into downloads, AiosPulse names the app and the funnel layer where the drop happened, instead of hiding it in a chart. Click the warning for the per-territory breakdown.

Which AI endpoints does the AI analyst support?

Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint: Zhipu GLM, DeepSeek, OpenAI, or a local Ollama instance. Your endpoint URL and key (if any) are stored locally; a local Ollama endpoint needs no key at all. The AI feature is entirely optional — every other view works without it.

Does AiosPulse work offline?

Yes. The app is local-first: sample data, settings and history are all on-disk. Network is used only when you explicitly collect from App Store Connect or call your own AI endpoint.

How do I delete my data?

Remove the API key in Settings (this deletes it from the Keychain) and delete the app. Collected data lives entirely inside the app's container; there is nothing stored anywhere else.
